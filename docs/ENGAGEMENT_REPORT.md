This is the closing report of the July 2026 rebuild engagement (Prompt.md section 19 deliverables). It is written incrementally as programmes close; the completion scorecard in section 10 is authoritative for what remains open. Baseline identity, gate evidence, and per-commit detail live in the git history of `rebuild/dngr-parity`; this report synthesises.

## 1. System purpose

Sirius renders black holes, wormholes, and warp-drive spacetimes by integrating photon geodesics through analytically specified metrics. The engagement's mandate: bring it to the fidelity standard of DNGR, the renderer DNEG and Kip Thorne built for Interstellar, as a ground-up C++26 rebuild that runs on every GPU vendor and down to 2 GB integrated graphics, on Windows, Linux, and WSL2.

## 2. Current-state assessment at engagement start

The July 2026 structural remediation had left a verified C++17 system: 757 tests (436 Mandatory) green, single authorities per concern, an accurate README, and a known ledger of deferred work. Its structural liabilities, confirmed by fresh investigation rather than inherited: a 6,561-line CUDA/OptiX device monolith carrying its own drifted copies of the physics (including two dead metric branches outside the closed registry); a CPU render path without the ray-bundle propagation that defines DNGR's approach; polarisation present only as unwired Stokes algebra; NVIDIA-only GPU support; a codename file scheme hostile to newcomers; and a build system whose forced binary directory broke preset builds.

## 3. Architecture summary (as rebuilt)

Seven layers, dependencies pointing downward only: `app` (CLI, config, viewer), `render` (session, tiles, writers, governor), `backend` (ComputeDevice seam; CPU tracer; Vulkan compute adapter), `kernels` (Slang single-source device physics), `core` (metrics, geodesics, tensors, disk, spectral, camera, constants), `oracle` (double-precision Boyer-Lindquist validation stack, deliberately off the render path), `base` (contracts, error channel). One kernel codebase in Slang compiles to SPIR-V for the Vulkan backend, which alone reaches AMD, Intel, and NVIDIA silicon plus Lavapipe software fallback (the WSL2 and CI path); the same source emits CUDA and Metal for future native adapters. The single authorities of the July remediation carried over intact, and the kernel-side metric catalogue can no longer diverge from the registry. `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` holds the full design and the old-name-to-new-name mapping.

## 4. Feature catalogue

Complete and live: nine-metric registry (asymptotically flat Kerr-Schild mass/spin/charge sectors plus the bounded spherical positive-Lambda de Sitter/Kottler sector; Morris-Thorne; Alcubierre); analytic metric derivatives with autodiff cross-checks; an adaptive Dormand-Prince RK45 Hamiltonian CPU tracer and adaptive RK4 step-doubling Slang tracer that rejects excessive local null drift and projects each accepted device tangent onto its continuous null-cone branch; an oracle-only implicit-midpoint/Yoshida stack with optional oracle-only null projection; Novikov-Thorne/Page-Thorne disk with blackbody spectral pipeline; Walker-Penrose polarisation transport on oracle and live paths; thin-lens/pinhole/fisheye cameras; spiral-tile threaded session; linear-EXR and PNG writers with one transfer encode each; a bounded film-inspired display finish; CLI with layered JSON/environment/flag configuration; OpenGL progressive viewer; install and CPack packaging.

Retired deliberately: CUDA/OptiX backend and PTX machinery (geodesic rendering gains nothing from triangle-BVH hardware; NVIDIA is served through Vulkan), Gödel and Taub-NUT dead kernel branches, the codename file scheme, nine legacy setup/benchmark scripts tied to the CUDA era.

## 5. Historical synthesis

The repository's recurring failure mode, documented across two engagements, is the additive parallel path: a second implementation of an existing concern that drifts until a bulk deletion. The July remediation answered it with single authorities; this rebuild answered it structurally, by making the GPU kernel a compilation product of one Slang source and the kernel's constant tables a generated artefact. Two latent defects surfaced as side-effects of raising the toolchain and adding physics, both fixed test-first: an FMA-sensitive discriminant in a deviation test (NaN under Clang), and hand-expanded Kerr Christoffel formulas in the oracle that disagreed with their own metric at O(1), unnoticed for a year because the integration path uses Hamiltonian derivatives. The lesson both encode: an untested algebraic transcription is a liability regardless of how authoritative its home file is, and a finite-difference pin against the defining object costs one test.

## 6. Target-state design

`docs/SPECIFICATION.md` defines it: parity criteria P1-P6 (geodesic accuracy, ray bundles by geodesic deviation, filtered star field, relativistic disk with a Doppler-suppression toggle, camera worldlines, HDR output discipline) and exceedance criteria E1-E4 (nine-metric catalogue, polarised transport, 2 GB-class hardware reach, a published verification estate). The engagement holds a hard behavioural invariant across the rebuild: every behaviour-preserving stage reproduces the pre-rebuild reference renders byte-for-byte, and every behaviour change lands as its own named, gated commit.

## 9. Remaining limitations

- (Resolved 2026-07-28.) Physical-GPU validation has now run on the named floor target, the AMD Radeon 780M, reached from WSL2 by building Mesa's Dozen driver (-Dvulkan-drivers=microsoft-experimental) over the WSLg D3D12 stack; stock Ubuntu ships no Dozen ICD, so VK_ICD_FILENAMES points the loader at the built manifest (route documented in scripts/validate-hardware.sh). Dozen presents the device as Vulkan 1.2, the SPIR-V 1.5 kernels load unmodified, and the AMD driver exposes shaderFloat64 through the translation, so every rung runs on silicon. The runbook passed end to end (log: renders/hardware-validation/validation-20260728-210540.log): full estate green on the real driver, the governed 2048 MiB IMAX-class render completed, and all three precision rungs rendered, with fp32 and fp32-comp bit-identical on hardware (confirming the Lavapipe finding on silicon) and the fp64 rung roughly twelve-fold slower than fp32, consistent with RDNA3's native double-precision rate. Two findings came out of the first run and are recorded: (1) a single dispatch covering a memory-governed tile exceeded the operating system's GPU watchdog and D3D12 removed the device, fixed by the dispatch governor (see section 7 addendum below); (2) one estate run crashed one fp64 kernel test with a bus error under full parallel load on the cold driver, unreproducible across four subsequent full-estate runs — recorded as a Dozen driver flake (Mesa labels the driver testing-only), not a Sirius defect. Performance through the D3D12 translation is indicative, not native; native Windows AMD Vulkan numbers remain the benchmark of record when that toolchain exists on the host.
- macOS is compile-targeted through MoltenVK but never executed here; the CI macOS job builds and runs the Correctness suites only.
- Reflection (P2996) and native contracts (P2900) remain toolchain-absent; the macro and codegen stand-ins are in place with recorded migration paths.
- The bounded volumetric closure, procedural turbulence, and film-inspired display finish remain explicitly non-GRMHD/non-stock models; the unrepresented corona declines and none of these effects expands the thin-disk physics claim.
- (Resolved post-closure, 2026-07-18.) The oracle's hand-coded component-wise Riemann method was incomplete off the diagonal. It was rebuilt test-first from the analytic metric derivatives, extended one order deeper (KerrMetricSecondDerivatives, pinned by finite differences of the first derivatives), and is now gated four ways: full-tensor agreement with finite differences of the connection, vacuum Ricci, the lowered-index symmetry group with first Bianchi, and Kretschmann by contraction. The contraction gate exposed a second latent defect: the closed-form Kretschmann's bracket carried (r² − a²cos²θ)² where Henry (2000, eq. 18) has (r² + a²cos²θ)², invisible wherever a·cosθ = 0 — exactly the regimes the historical gates sampled, including a circular off-axis pin that recomputed the same expression. Both are fixed; the live-path Kretschmann pin now extends off-equator for Kerr a=0.9.
- (Resolved post-closure, 2026-07-18.) The CPU/Vulkan shadow-fraction gap (0.24 versus 0.11 on the parity scene) was not the integrator-method difference it was recorded as: it was the CPU trace loop terminating rays at their first RK45 step rejection, which near-critical photon-ring rays always hit, shading them into the shadow band. With rejections retried (see the Morris-Thorne entry above), the measured gap is ~1e-3 and the parity bound tightened from 0.25 to 0.02. The paths still integrate by different methods by design; that difference proves to be worth ~1e-3 of shadow fraction, not 0.13.
- (Resolved post-closure, 2026-07-18.) Morris-Thorne now renders on the CPU path through MorrisThorneCartesian, a flat-plus-rank-one Cartesian embedding of the spherical family (g_ij = delta_ij + f n_i n_j, f = b/(r-b), Sherman-Morrison closed-form inverse); the spherical family remains the shape authority and the kernel-parity reference. One sheet, throat as capture surface (recorded decision: the far mouth's universe is unmodelled, so the throat renders dark). Gated by chart-agreement, derivative, inverse, and capture-surface suites, an Ellis-deflection tracer gate (quadratic falloff, Chetouani-Clement leading order within 50%), and an end-to-end session probe. Wiring it exposed a live-path defect: the CPU trace loop treated an RK45 step rejection as ray termination (outcome MaxSteps, shaded near-black). No Kerr-Schild session scene triggered a rejection at session tolerances until the strong-field region of the 512x512 tape geometry did; the wormhole triggered it at first approach. Rejected steps now retry at the reduced step (still counted against max_steps). The fix moved 16.1% of pixels in the pinned Kerr scene, all inside the shadow/photon-ring bounding box with the far field byte-identical, and left Alcubierre byte-identical; the baseline tapes were regenerated with documented provenance (renders/rebuild-baseline/README.md). The fp64 kernel rung is now real (same session, later on 2026-07-18): the single trace source compiles twice, with SIRIUS_FP64 widening the Cartesian trajectory core (gr_types Real) to double — 2,581 double-typed instructions in trace_fp64.spv against zero in trace.spv. GLSL.std.450 restricts trig/exp/pow to 32-bit floats, so the disk shading, starfield projection, and warp bubble-wall profile evaluate in float at explicitly cast boundaries, none of which feed back into the trajectory for the Kerr-Schild family. SIRIUS_PRECISION=fp64 selects the rung on shaderFloat64 devices and still declines loudly on devices without it; gated on Lavapipe by kernel agreement (mean |d| 6.4e-8 against fp32 on the Kerr scene) and an end-to-end session render. The ladder's middle rung (fp32 with double-single compensated accumulations) remains open.

- (Owner-directed close-out, 2026-07-18, second session.) Three further milestones landed. Vulkan auto GO-LIVE: backend `auto` now resolves to Vulkan when a device is present and the registry marks the metric gpu-dispatchable, with a logged CPU fallback otherwise; `vulkan` joined the accepted backend names; `--cpu` pins the reference path and the baseline tapes (verified byte-identical under the flag). GPU Morris-Thorne: the trace kernel dispatches the wormhole (id 3) through the same Cartesian embedding the CPU renders, gated by a CPU-versus-Vulkan statistical parity test that measured identical throat-shadow fractions and 2.4e-7 mean background luminance difference on Lavapipe. Precision-ladder middle rung: trace_fp32comp.spv carries Kahan-compensated state accumulation (SIRIUS_PRECISION=fp32-comp, any device), completing the three-rung ladder of ARCHITECTURE.md section 6; on Lavapipe its output is bit-identical to plain fp32 (IEEE-exact software arithmetic leaves nothing to recover at these step counts) while the gate bounds it against the fp64 reference for drivers where fp32 is sloppier. The specification's pre-hardware go-live guardrail is recorded as superseded in section 1.5.

- (Addendum to section 7, 2026-07-28.) The dispatch governor (src/sirius/render/dispatch_governor.h) joined the render layer as the memory governor's time-domain companion, added when the first physical-GPU run showed the two constraints are independent: the 2864x2864 governed tile fit the 2 GiB residency budget and still died with D3D12 device removal, because one compute dispatch outlived the OS GPU watchdog (Windows TDR, ~2 s) that software Vulkan never enforces. Tiles now submit as adaptive row bands targeting a wall-time budget per dispatch (default 250 ms; SIRIUS_DISPATCH_TARGET_MS overrides, 0 restores one dispatch per tile). The trace kernel addresses pixels absolutely, so banding is value-transparent by construction; growth is damped at two-fold per step while shrink is unbounded, because overshoot risks device removal and undershoot only costs submission overhead. The controller's contract carries nine postcondition tests (DispatchGovernor suite), and the estate rose 783 to 792, green on GCC 14 and Clang 21 with zero warnings.

- (Adversarial review cycle, 2026-07-28, owner-directed.) The release increment e233f13..5d6762a went through a Bun-style adversarial review: two independent reviewers received only the diff with instructions to assume it wrong, and every finding faced a refutation verifier that had to demonstrate the failure concretely before it counted. Ten findings emerged; three were refuted (two duplicate stale-count claims about this report, one test-coverage claim), and seven — collapsing to four distinct defects — were confirmed with executable evidence, all in the dispatch governor landed earlier the same day. The two serious ones shared a root the implementer's own review missed: the controller reasoned in rows while dispatch cost scales with band area, and fed back its desired height rather than the work actually dispatched, so every tile's truncated tail band ratcheted the next band up by the full growth cap (measured: a systematic 2x-target overshoot at tile boundaries), and a height learned on a narrow last-column tile carried into the next full-width tile, where a verifier demonstrated the whole-tile dispatch shape the governor exists to prevent. The controller now learns a band area and attributes every measurement to the pixels the dispatch actually covered, which closes both routes; two regression tests pin the exact demonstrated scenarios. The minor findings are also fixed: SIRIUS_DISPATCH_TARGET_MS now rejects non-finite and overflowing values loudly (inf and 1e400 previously disabled banding silently while the log claimed it active), and the band-dispatch count is surfaced in the session completion log rather than being recorded and read by nothing. Estate 792 to 796 enabled across the cycle, 476 Mandatory unchanged, green on both compilers; re-validated on the physical 780M with a six-tile governed render (remainder column and row included) completing in 77 governed dispatches with no watchdog event.
